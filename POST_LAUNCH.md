# POST_LAUNCH.md

What is deferred until after launch, moved out of ROADMAP.md on 2026-10-19 (QA part 4). **None of it is reachable in the app**: no button, row, tab or link leads to
it, and no screen says it is coming. APP_RULES.md R19 keeps ROADMAP.md for anything that *is* reachable and unbuilt; this file is for what is not in the app at all.
A feature moves from here into the app only with its rules, its tests and its privacy review (the inventory, the Privacy Center, the manifest and PRIVACY_REPORT.md
where data is involved).

## Deferred features

- Name in Home's greeting. The name set on Profile is shown on Profile only; Home's greeting does not use it yet.
- Sleep share card. Home's "Share this night" card stays the only share.
- Recorded, voice-guided meditations. Meditation reads its lines in the system voice, and only if asked. Recorded guides need recordings (and the rights to a voice) and are a decision of their own: R31 keeps audio files out of the sleep sounds, and a recording is a different thing from a sound.
- Adaptive wind-down lengths. The length is 10, 20 or 30 minutes, the last one chosen is remembered. Suggesting a length from the night's bedtime plan (when the evening reminder fires, how long until bed) is not built.
- Recovery share card. Home's "Share this night" card stays the only share.
- Recovery load factor from workouts. The model has four factors (sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, consistency); training load isn't one until workouts are read and tested.
- Activity insights. Nothing about workouts or steps is generated until workout data is read and the rules are tested (Recovery's load factor waits on the same thing).
- Insight share card. Home's "Share this night" card stays the only share.
- Remote model (`aiRemote`). `RemoteModelProvider`, its request and response types and its privacy filter exist and are tested; there is no transport, no endpoint and no key, and the flag is off. Nothing leaves the device until a transport, the consent screen and the privacy review exist.
- Coach history sync. Coach's conversations (`AIConversation`, `AIMessage`) stay on this device: no CloudKit, no backup. Syncing them across devices needs its own privacy decision and an encrypted zone.
- Localised Coach and crisis resources. The router's patterns, every scripted and template sentence, and the crisis reply (findahelpline.com) are English only; each language needs its own patterns, copy, banned-word list and a local emergency resource before Coach is offered in it.
- Coach share. Nothing in Coach can be shared; Home's "Share this night" card stays the only share.
- Voice input. The text field takes typing only.
- Bedtime in Coach and on the tab accessory. Tonight, Home's Tonight block and evening hero, Profile and the evening reminder read one recommendation (`BedtimeInputsBuilder.recommendation`, by plan: R61). The tab accessory and Coach's "When should I sleep?" still show wake time less sleep need, so they can differ from Tonight's window for Premium when the catch-up or the gradual limit applies. They should read the same recommendation.
- Adaptive sleep need. The sleep need is the number the person set; nothing estimates it from their nights.
- Advanced alarm features. The alarm is entirely free (R57) and has repeat days and the system's snooze; anything beyond that (a gentler ramp, a second alarm, different tones per day) is not designed. If any of it becomes Premium it is added to the feature table first.
- A yearly review. Needs a year of data (a calendar year's 12 monthly reviews, or 365 nights); not designed. Weekly and monthly reviews are built (R63), so the feature table lists the weekly cover, the full weekly review and the monthly review; a yearly one is not listed and no screen or paywall may promise it.
- Smart Wake and haptic wake (research only). NOCTIS has no sleep-stage wake-up and the copy never claims one. A Watch app exists now, but it reads no Health data and runs no engine (R50), so it cannot detect a stage; a wake-up that uses the Watch's sleep tracking and a haptic on the wrist would need a Watch-side session during the night, Health read access on the Watch and a decision about what is claimed. Until that is researched and tested on a real Watch nothing is built and nothing is claimed.
- Live Activity for the wake window. It needs Smart Wake (a sleep-stage wake-up from the Apple Watch), which NOCTIS does not have, so it is not built. The Live Activities NOCTIS has are the wind-down's (R49) and the alarm's own snooze countdown, drawn by `NoctisAlarmWidget` for AlarmKit. Decided not to build one for the morning summary or for the alarm (R49).
- Needs licensed recordings. Forest and Night sounds: they can't be synthesized convincingly, so they wait for real recordings (and their licences). Every sound that is in the app is made in code (R31).
- A Home tab accessory for the sound that is playing. Starting and stopping a sound from Control Center, Siri and Shortcuts is done (R47, R48); the tab accessory is not.
- Apple Watch, what is built and what is not. The Watch app shows the iPhone's summary on five pages (R50, R51), has four complications (R54) and breathing (R55), the wind-down Live Activity has a small presentation for the Smart Stack (R56), and Settings has an Apple Watch page (R29, R52). Not built: a wind-down session on the Watch itself (below), Smart Wake (below), and anything that needs the Watch to read Health (R50).
- Writing Mindful Minutes to Health. Breathing and Meditation (on the iPhone, and on the Watch when it has breathing) save nothing to Apple Health: NOCTIS reads Health and never writes it (the data inventory holds it to `toShare: []`). Writing a mindful session needs write authorization for that one type, a change to the data inventory (`writes`), the Privacy Center, the manifest and `PRIVACY_REPORT.md`, and the owner's review of the wording.
- A Readiness page on the Watch. Not designed: Recovery is NOCTIS's one measure of how ready the person is, and it is the Watch's first page; a separate page would repeat it unless it said something different (for example today's plan set against last night).
- A standalone Watch wind-down with sounds. The wind-down, Sleep sounds and Meditation run on the iPhone. The Watch has no wind-down session of its own and plays no sound: audio on the Watch (its speaker or Bluetooth headphones), a session that lasts with the wrist down and the mixing with the iPhone's own sound would each need design and a real Watch to test on.
- Language. The app ships in English only, so Settings has no Language row. It appears by itself (`LanguageRow.isOffered`) once a second localization is in the bundle; Coach's own language work is listed above.
- The paywall sheets (from a locked thing, from Settings) reuse onboarding's paywall as it is: its dismiss button reads "Continue with the free plan" and its hero is the onboarding one. A wording written for someone already using the app is not.
- iCloud sync (not built). Nothing syncs: no CloudKit, no iCloud key-value store, no iCloud entitlement, and every `ModelConfiguration` has `cloudKitDatabase: .none`. Syncing the profile, preferences or Coach history needs its own privacy decision, a change to the inventory (`syncs`), the Privacy Center's "What syncs" section, the manifest and `PRIVACY_REPORT.md`, and an encrypted zone for anything the person wrote.
- Sinhala and Tamil translations. The app ships in English only. Both need a native speaker's translation, not a machine's, including the privacy statements (generated from the inventory), the Help answers and Coach's scripted lines; Coach's own language work is listed above.
- Configurable widgets. The widgets are static: no choice of what they show (an `AppIntentConfiguration` could let the person pick, for example, recovery or bedtime for a small widget), and no buttons in them. Larger families (extra large on iPad, StandBy tuning) are not designed. The two controls (R48) are not configurable either.
- Smart Stack suggestions beyond relevance scores. The widgets give the system a relevance score and the wake-up and bedtime contexts; nothing donates intents or suggests widgets from what the person does, and the App Shortcuts are not shown as suggestions by NOCTIS itself.
- Plan expiry on the widgets, the Watch and the intents. They learn the plan from the snapshot the app writes, which happens when the app sees the plan change (a purchase, an update while it runs, a launch). A subscription that ends while the app is not running keeps its Premium widgets, complications and Siri phrases until the app next runs. Options: have the Health background wake ask the App Store before it writes, and/or carry the period's end date in the snapshot so surfaces lock themselves. Not built; needs a real device and a real expiry to test.
- A monthly review notification. Not added: only the weekly one exists (its text is still generic, R13, and it opens the latest weekly review). Whether a month deserves its own reminder, and when, needs a decision; until then the monthly review is found on Home for the first three days of a month and in Insights.
- Coach reading reviews. Coach cannot read the reviews (the data inventory says so, R63). Letting it explain a week or a month from its `ReviewRecord` (derived values only) would need a decision, a change to the inventory's Coach column, the Privacy Center and `PRIVACY_REPORT.md`.
- Sharing a review beyond what is on screen. Sharing a review would show only what the page shows (as the Home share cards do); a share that carries more than is visible, or a link to a review, is not designed.
- A personal records page. A month's longest night and highest recovery are in the monthly review; a page of the person's all-time records beyond the Insights records is not designed.
- The review insights page and Insights found late. A review points to the insights NOCTIS found while its week or month was under way; an insight found after the review was made (a person who opens the app only on the review day) is not in a review that is already frozen. Not designed: whether a review should look again inside its first 24 hours for insights found since (it does for nights, R63).
- Widgets, the Watch and the intents still learn the plan from the snapshot the app writes. The app itself now asks the store again when the plan's end passes while it is open (QA-127), but a plan that ends while the app is not running still waits for the next launch to reach them (see "Plan expiry on the widgets, the Watch and the intents" above).

## Checks that need a real device or a Sandbox account

These are checks, not features; QA_REPORT.md section 5 is the same list for the release.

- Verify the Watch on a real Apple Watch. Pairing and installing NOCTIS from the Watch app; the morning summary arriving overnight without opening the iPhone app; the Watch showing yesterday's numbers as "Updated Nh ago" and never as today's; "Ask iPhone to update" with the iPhone reachable and not; "Delete all NOCTIS data" on the iPhone wiping the Watch (including one that was away); Health numbers drawn as a lock while the Watch is locked; the five pages and their Digital Crown scrolling on a real case size; Settings > Apple Watch on an iPhone with a Watch paired, with the Watch app installed and not.
- Verify background delivery on a real iPhone. HealthKit background delivery is best-effort and cannot be exercised in the simulator: confirm an overnight sleep sample refreshes the morning widget without opening the app, with and without a paired Apple Watch, and read the `BackgroundRefresh` signposts.
- Verify the controls and intents on a real iPhone. Starting the sleep sound from the Sleep sounds control with the screen locked (and how long the system keeps the app alive to do it), the toggle after the system ends NOCTIS while a sound plays with no timer, each Siri phrase, the unlock before Recovery and Sleep are spoken, and adding both controls to Control Center are for an iPhone: the simulator has no Siri, no Lock Screen audio launch and no real Action button.
- Verify the Watch complications on a real Apple Watch. Adding each of the four kinds in each family to a face (the corner's curved gauge and label can only draw on a face, not in the simulator's gallery); a tinted face and the accented rendering (the gallery only approximates it); Health values drawn as "Unlock to see" while the Watch is locked; the Smart Stack putting Recovery, Sleep and Sleep quality forward in the morning and Bedtime in the evening; the morning summary reaching the complications overnight without opening either app (R52's transfer budget).
- Verify breathing on a real Apple Watch. The mindfulness extended runtime session keeping the exercise running with the wrist down and the screen asleep (and for how long: the system ends it at its time limit, about ten minutes, and the 10 minute Box and Relax exercises run a few seconds past that, where the Watch pauses with "Raise your wrist to continue"); the feel of the direction-up, click, direction-down and success haptics; the session ending when the Digital Crown or another app takes the screen; Reduce Motion on the Watch.
- Verify the wind-down Live Activity on a real Apple Watch. The small presentation in the Smart Stack while a wind-down runs on the iPhone (its layout, that it ticks, and that it leaves when the session ends); the simulator's Smart Stack does not show it (R56).
- Verify gating on a real iPhone and Watch. A locked widget opening the paywall sheet; a purchase while a widget is on the Home Screen (does it unlock without opening the app?); each Siri phrase on both plans (the free "Wind is part of NOCTIS Premium" reply, the bedtime window for Premium); the Watch's locked Heart and Tonight pages and locked complications, and a lapsed subscription locking the surfaces again.
- Verify the reviews on a real iPhone. The weekly notification tapped from the Lock Screen (app closed, in the background, free plan) opening the latest weekly review; Home's review card appearing on the review day and going after three days or when opened; the share sheet from a review (the card's 1080x1350 image reaching Messages, Photos and Files, and "Include details" off by default); a purchase from the weekly cover's locked card unlocking the story in place (the page the person is on stays); a week that crosses a daylight saving change; a change of time zone on the review day; swiping, the left and right thirds of the screen and Back/Next on a real case size.
- Real-device and Sandbox checks left by QA part 3 (QA_REPORT.md section 5): purchases with a Sandbox tester (declined Ask to Buy, billing retry and grace, a real expiry, Family Sharing), the Apple Watch pairing and overnight delivery, Health background delivery, a delivered notification tapped cold and warm, Airplane mode, Low Power Mode and a full disk, file protection classes on a locked device, Instruments on a device, and a signed archive.
