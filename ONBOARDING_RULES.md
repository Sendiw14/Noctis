# ONBOARDING_RULES.md (v2, Ink & Ivory)
Read before touching any onboarding screen. Check every screen against every rule.

Direction: ALL onboarding screens, including Welcome, share one flat ivory paper language — NoctisPaper background, cocoa ink, solid flat accent colors, NoctisCard, NoctisIconTile, NoctisCTA. No dark screens, no gradients, no glow, no starfield, no atmosphere mesh anywhere in onboarding. R1-R13 apply to Welcome exactly as they apply to every other step.

R1 Full-bleed paper. PaperBackground (ivory + 3% grain tile) is the root: ZStack { PaperBackground().ignoresSafeArea(.all); content }. Only content respects safe areas. No bands top or bottom. Hosting view background = ivory.
R2 Flat and honest. Solid flat shapes only. No glow, blur, aurora, gradient fills on content, or glass on content. Allowed: bottom-stack fade, iOS 26 glass on the back button only (fallback: white circle + hairline), one soft shadow spec (cocoa .06, blur 24, y 8) on floating cards. Nothing clips: no visible rectangles, no hard-edged bands.
R3 Titles never truncate. 3 lines max, minimumScaleFactor .8, fixedSize(horizontal:false, vertical:true). Titles take their natural height; content starts exactly 24pt below the title.
R4 Type. SF Pro Rounded everywhere (.fontDesign(.rounded)). Title 38 bold, tracking -0.6, one emphasized phrase in orangeText. Hero numerals 64-88 heavy, monospacedDigit, contentTransition(.numericText()). Body 17 ink2. Caption 13 ink3. Min text 12. Never italic serif. Dynamic Type: all onboarding text scales. Each size above is its value at the default text size (Large) and is anchored to a text style: titles and hero numerals → .largeTitle; body 17, row titles and CTA labels → .body; labels (14–16) → .subheadline; captions 13 → .footnote; small print ≤12 → .caption/.caption2. Hero numerals use Font.system(size:weight:design:) with @ScaledMetric relative to .largeTitle, capped at 1.6x, and shrink to fit a card that still can't hold them. Icons, tiles and marks beside text scale with @ScaledMetric relative to that text, capped at 1.6x; decorative hero graphics stay fixed. At accessibility sizes, rows that sit side by side stack vertically (Welcome trust card, Plan columns, Sample legend, Goal rows, badge rows), and labels wrap rather than truncate. Use noctisFont / noctisNumeral / noctisIcon (DesignSystem/NoctisScaledType.swift).
R5 Palette (NoctisPaper tokens only, no hardcoded hex in views). bg #F8F5EE, surface #FFFFFF, hairline #E9E3D8, track #ECE6DA, ink #2B1D14, ink2 #6B5B4E, ink3 #7A6B5E, orangeText #C2571A. Fills: sage #9DB073, orange #E07B39, butter #F2CE6B, lavender #A08CEB, cocoa #4A2F22. Stage colors: Deep cocoa, Core orange, REM sage, Awake butter. Text on cocoa/ink fills is ivory, on other fills ink. Unit test contrast: ink, ink2, ink3 on bg >= 4.5; orangeText on bg >= 3 (display only); ivory on ink >= 4.5.
R6 One hero per screen. Every screen with a title starts its content exactly 24pt below the title, on every screen, no exceptions. Never vertically center the content region. Leftover space collects above the bottom stack. Only Welcome (no title) centers its content block optically (1 part above, 2 below). At most 24pt between hero, caption and CTA.
R7 Top bar on steps 1-6, same position on every screen (safe-area top + 8pt): 44pt back button + 6 segments (4pt tall, 4pt gaps, track color, cocoa fill wiping in 0.4s). Nothing else in that row. Hidden on Welcome, Plan build phase and Paywall.
R8 Layout is system layout. Use VStack, Grid, ScrollView, ViewThatFits. No .position(), and no GeometryReader-driven absolute placement outside Canvas. No debug overlays, borders, coordinate labels or test colors, ever. Before reporting done run: grep -rn "\.position(\|\.border(\|DEBUG" Packages/NoctisKit/Sources/OnboardingFeature and justify or remove every hit.
R9 Motion budget. One hero animation per screen (<= 1.2s). Everything else is a 12pt rise with 60ms stagger (.settle). Reduce Motion: 0.2s fades only. Haptics via NoctisHaptics only.
R10 CTA. Cocoa capsule, 58pt (at the default text size; it grows with its label, never clipping it), ivory 17 semibold label + trailing arrow.right, press = scale .97 with spring return, loading = label morphs to a ring, disabled = track fill + ink3 label. Secondary = 44pt text button in ink2. Bottom stack pinned with safeAreaInset(.bottom) over a paper fade so scrolling content slides under it. At AX sizes content scrolls and the CTA stays pinned.
R11 Self-verify. After each screen: build, run on iPhone 17 Pro, capture with `xcrun simctl io booted screenshot /tmp/<screen>-<variant>.png`, then LOOK at the images. Variants: 17 Pro, iPhone SE, AX3 (`xcrun simctl ui booted content_size accessibility-extra-large`), plus every state named in the screen prompt. Fix until R1-R10 pass. Report one pass/fail line per rule. Never say done without screenshots.
R12 Performance. 60fps. One TimelineView per screen at most. Pause when scenePhase != .active or Reduce Motion.
R13 Honesty. Never fabricate health data. Sample data is always labeled "Sample". No fake urgency, scarcity or countdowns. Paywall shows real StoreKit prices; close and Restore are always visible.
R14 Scroll edges. (a) ScrollView must never clip card shadows: use .scrollClipDisabled() with horizontal padding inside the scroll content (or contentMargins). No shaded rectangle may appear behind card groups. (b) The bottom fade (24pt paper-color gradient above the CTA stack) renders ONLY when content actually extends below the visible area; the top fade (24pt paper-color gradient under a pinned header/title) renders ONLY when content has scrolled up under it. Drive both with onScrollGeometryChange. When content fits, there is no fade. Neither fade may show a grey band, and a partially scrolled-away card must fade out completely (including its shadow) rather than leave a sliver. The last piece of content clears the CTA stack by 16pt when fully scrolled. Text may never render half-faded at rest. At accessibility sizes, stacked text blocks sit at least 28pt apart (fade + 4) so a resting fade can always fall between them.
R15 Consistency. NoctisCard inner padding is always 20pt. Stacked cards are always 12pt apart. One documented exception, the compact card (`NoctisPaperCard(density: .compact)`): 14pt vertical padding (horizontal stays 20pt) and 8pt between stacked cards, allowed only on Health and Notifications so they fit per R16. Goal names use sentence case everywhere ("Better sleep", "More energy").
R16 Fit. On iPhone 17 Pro at default text size, Welcome, Rhythm, Sample, Health, Notifications and Plan must fit with NO scrolling. Tighten spacing to achieve this; never cut content. Only Goal and Paywall may scroll.
R17 Digits. monospacedDigit only on numbers that animate. Static text like "Bedtime around 11:00 PM" uses proportional digits.
