# ROADMAP.md
Unbuilt features (APP_RULES.md R19). Each one shows "Coming soon" or is not reachable until built. This file must be empty before release.

- Name in Home's greeting. The name set on Profile is shown on Profile only; Home's greeting does not use it yet.
- Free vs Premium gating for Sleep: Month, the quality factors and the 14-night picker. All of Sleep is open until the paywall decides.
- Sleep share card. Home's "Share this night" card stays the only share.
- Recorded, voice-guided meditations. Meditation reads its lines in the system voice, and only if asked. Recorded guides need recordings (and the rights to a voice) and are a decision of their own: R31 keeps audio files out of the sleep sounds, and a recording is a different thing from a sound.
- Wind-down Live Activity and Dynamic Island (an ActivityKit pass). The countdown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while a session runs: one `Activity` started with the session and ended with it, showing the planned end with `Text(timerInterval:)` so nothing is updated every second, in the night colors, with no controls that need the app to be open. Not started: it needs a widget extension target, the `NSSupportsLiveActivities` key and a design for the compact and minimal presentations.
- Wind-down Control Center control. An iOS 18 `ControlWidget` that begins a wind-down (and one that plays a chosen sleep sound). Not started: it needs the same widget extension and the App Intents below.
- App Intents. `StartWindDownIntent` (opens `noctis://wind-down?autostart=1`'s equivalent, for Siri, Shortcuts, the Action button and Spotlight) and `PlaySleepSoundsIntent` (a chosen sound, optionally with a timer). `AppRoute.windDown(autostart: true)` is the Siri-ready entry they will call; neither intent exists yet.
- Free vs Premium gating for the wind-down. The spec makes Wind Down premium: the session, Breathing, Meditation and Reflect, and the sound under it. All of it is open until the paywall decides.
- Adaptive wind-down lengths. The length is 10, 20 or 30 minutes, the last one chosen is remembered. Suggesting a length from the night's bedtime plan (when the evening reminder fires, how long until bed) is not built.
- Free vs Premium gating for Recovery: free is Today and 7D; premium is 30D, 90D and each factor's history. All of Recovery is open until the paywall decides.
- Recovery share card. Home's "Share this night" card stays the only share.
- Recovery load factor from workouts. The model has four factors (sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, consistency); training load isn't one until workouts are read and tested.
- Free vs Premium gating for Insights: free is the featured story plus one recent insight; premium is everything (the rest of Recent, Your records, History, every detail). All of Insights is open until the paywall decides.
- Activity insights. Nothing about workouts or steps is generated until workout data is read and the rules are tested (Recovery's load factor waits on the same thing).
- Insight share card. Home's "Share this night" card stays the only share.
- Remote model (`aiRemote`). `RemoteModelProvider`, its request and response types and its privacy filter exist and are tested; there is no transport, no endpoint and no key, and the flag is off. Nothing leaves the device until a transport, the consent screen and the privacy review exist.
- Coach history sync. Coach's conversations (`AIConversation`, `AIMessage`) stay on this device: no CloudKit, no backup. Syncing them across devices needs its own privacy decision and an encrypted zone.
- Free vs Premium gating for Coach. Proposal: free is 3 questions a day (the suggested questions and typed ones count alike; a fixed reply does not); premium is unlimited questions and History. All of Coach is open until the paywall decides.
- Localised Coach and crisis resources. The router's patterns, every scripted and template sentence, and the crisis reply (findahelpline.com) are English only; each language needs its own patterns, copy, banned-word list and a local emergency resource before Coach is offered in it.
- Coach share. Nothing in Coach can be shared; Home's "Share this night" card stays the only share.
- Voice input. The text field takes typing only.
- Bedtime on Home and in Coach. Tonight's window comes from `BedtimeRecommendationEngine` (bedtime-v1); Home's Tonight block, the evening hero and the tab accessory, and Coach's "When should I sleep?", still show wake time minus sleep need, so they can differ from Tonight when the catch-up or the gradual limit applies. They should read the same recommendation.
- Adaptive sleep need. The sleep need is the number the person set; nothing estimates it from their nights.
- Free vs Premium gating for the alarm. Advanced alarm features (repeat days, snooze) are open until the paywall decides.
- Smart Wake and haptic wake. NOCTIS has no sleep-stage wake-up and the copy never claims one: it needs the Apple Watch.
- Live Activity for the wake window. The only Live Activity is the alarm's own snooze countdown, drawn by `NoctisAlarmWidget` for AlarmKit.
- Needs licensed recordings. Forest and Night sounds: they can't be synthesized convincingly, so they wait for real recordings (and their licences). Every sound that is in the app is made in code (R31).
- Premium sound packs. Free vs Premium gating for Sleep sounds: all six are open until the paywall decides.
- Control Center control and the Play Sleep Sounds intent. Starting and stopping a sound from Control Center, Siri and Shortcuts (App Intents), and a Home tab accessory for the sound that is playing (a system pass).
- Apple Watch and widgets. NOCTIS has no Apple Watch app and no home screen widgets (the alarm's Live Activity belongs to AlarmKit). Settings has no row for either (R36).
- Language. The app ships in English only, so Settings has no Language row. It appears by itself (`LanguageRow.isOffered`) once a second localization is in the bundle; Coach's own language work is listed above.
- Real support, terms and privacy addresses. `StoreLegalLinks` holds placeholders: `support@noctis.app`, `https://noctis.app/terms` and `https://noctis.app/privacy`. Contact support, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy in Settings use them; swap them for the published ones before release.
- Upgrade from Settings reuses onboarding's paywall as it is (its dismiss button reads "Continue with limited version"). A wording for someone already using the app is not written.
- iCloud sync (not built). Nothing syncs: no CloudKit, no iCloud key-value store, no iCloud entitlement, and every `ModelConfiguration` has `cloudKitDatabase: .none`. Syncing the profile, preferences or Coach history needs its own privacy decision, a change to the inventory (`syncs`), the Privacy Center's "What syncs" section, the manifest and `PRIVACY_REPORT.md`, and an encrypted zone for anything the person wrote.
- Sinhala and Tamil translations. The app ships in English only. Both need a native speaker's translation, not a machine's, including the privacy statements (generated from the inventory), the Help answers and Coach's scripted lines; Coach's own language work is listed above.
- Pseudo-localization and right-to-left checks, in the final QA pass: a pseudo-language build (longer strings, accents) and a right-to-left run (Arabic or Hebrew layout) to find clipped text, mirrored-layout mistakes and strings that are not in the catalogs.
- Terms of Use and Privacy Policy URLs, and the support address, supplied by the owner. `StoreLegalLinks` holds placeholders; App Store Connect needs a real Privacy Policy URL, and the published policy must agree with `PRIVACY_REPORT.md` and the Privacy Center. The owner also reviews every privacy statement's wording before release.
- Free vs Premium gating pass. One pass over the whole app to decide, screen by screen, what is free and what is Premium (Sleep: Month and the quality factors, Recovery: 30D and 90D, Insights, Coach's daily questions, the wind-down, the alarm's advanced features, the sound packs), and then gate them with one entitlement check and one paywall wording. Everything is open until it is done.
- Free vs Premium gating for widgets. Proposal: free is the small Home Screen widget; premium is medium, large and the Lock Screen widgets. All of them are open until the paywall decides.
- Widget configuration and interaction. The widgets are static: no choice of what they show, no buttons, no Control Center control. Larger families (extra large on iPad, StandBy tuning) are not designed.
- Verify background delivery on a real iPhone. HealthKit background delivery is best-effort and cannot be exercised in the simulator: confirm an overnight sleep sample refreshes the morning widget without opening the app, with and without a paired Apple Watch, and read the `BackgroundRefresh` signposts.
