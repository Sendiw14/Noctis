# APP_RULES.md (v1, main app)
Read before touching any main-app screen (everything after onboarding). Check every screen against every rule.

Scope: R1-R17 are copied from ONBOARDING_RULES.md (v2, Ink & Ivory). Changes are marked **(changed)**. Clauses that name onboarding screens (Welcome, Rhythm, Sample, Health, Notifications, Plan, Goal, Paywall) describe onboarding and are kept only for reference; ONBOARDING_RULES.md still governs onboarding, which stays light-only. R18-R20 are new.

Platform **(new)**: Xcode 27 with the iOS 27 SDK. Minimum deployment target iOS 26, onboarding included. No iOS 18 code paths: no `#available(iOS 26, *)` branches and no material fallbacks for glass. Guard iOS 27-only APIs with `#available(iOS 27, *)` and keep a plain iOS 26 behavior beside them. Zero warnings in Debug, Release and the test build.

Direction: the main app shares onboarding's flat paper language — paper background, ink text, solid flat accent colors, NoctisCard, NoctisIconTile, NoctisCTA — in system Light AND Dark. No gradients, no glow, no starfield, no atmosphere mesh.

R1 Full-bleed paper. PaperBackground (ivory + 3% grain tile) is the root: ZStack { PaperBackground().ignoresSafeArea(.all); content }. Only content respects safe areas. No bands top or bottom. Hosting view background = ivory. **(changed)** In Dark the paper is the dark `bg` token; "ivory" above means the current mode's `bg`.
R2 Flat and honest. Solid flat shapes only. No glow, blur, aurora, gradient fills on content, or glass on content. Allowed: bottom-stack fade, Liquid Glass on the back button only **(changed: no pre-iOS 26 fallback)**, one soft shadow spec (cocoa .06, blur 24, y 8) on floating cards. R3 Titles never truncate. 3 lines max, minimumScaleFactor .8, fixedSize(horizontal:false, vertical:true). Titles take their natural height; content starts exactly 24pt below the title.
R4 Type. SF Pro Rounded everywhere (.fontDesign(.rounded)). Title 38 bold, tracking -0.6, one emphasized phrase in orangeText. Hero numerals 64-88 heavy, monospacedDigit, contentTransition(.numericText()). Body 17 ink2. Caption 13 ink3. Min text 12. Never italic serif. Dynamic Type: all text scales. Each size above is its value at the default text size (Large) and is anchored to a text style: titles and hero numerals → .largeTitle; body 17, row titles and CTA labels → .body; labels (14–16) → .subheadline; captions 13 → .footnote; small print ≤12 → .caption/.caption2. Hero numerals use Font.system(size:weight:design:) with @ScaledMetric relative to .largeTitle, capped at 1.6x, and shrink to fit a card that still can't hold them. Icons, tiles and marks beside text scale with @ScaledMetric relative to that text, capped at 1.6x; decorative hero graphics stay fixed. At accessibility sizes, rows that sit side by side stack vertically (Welcome trust card, Plan columns, Sample legend, Goal rows, badge rows), and labels wrap rather than truncate. Use noctisFont / noctisNumeral / noctisIcon (DesignSystem/NoctisScaledType.swift).

Pause when scenePhase != .active or Reduce Motion. **(changed, Home v4)** The main app has exactly one `TimelineView`: the evening strip's "now" marker. R13 Honesty. Never fabricate health data. Sample data is always labeled "Sample". No fake urgency, scarcity or countdowns. Paywall shows real StoreKit prices; close and Restore are always visible.
R14 Scroll edges. **(changed)** Content never renders behind the status bar: main-app scroll views without a navigation bar use `noctisStatusBarGuard()`. That is a paper scrim, solid under the status bar and fading out over 16pt, and it is the only status-bar treatment; the system top scroll edge effect is hidden beneath it. (a) ScrollView must never clip card shadows: use .scrollClipDisabled() with horizontal padding inside the scroll content (or contentMargins). No shaded rectangle may appear behind card groups. (b) The bottom fade (24pt paper-color gradient above the CTA stack) renders ONLY when content actually extends below the visible area; the top fade (24pt paper-color gradient under a pinned header/title) renders ONLY when content has scrolled up under it. Drive both with onScrollGeometryChange. When content fits, there is no fade. Neither fade may show a grey band, and a partially scrolled-away card must fade out completely (including its shadow) rather than leave a sliver. The last piece of content clears the CTA stack by 16pt when fully scrolled. Text may never render half-faded at rest. At accessibility sizes, stacked text blocks sit at least 28pt apart (fade + 4) so a resting fade can always fall between them.

Color blocks (Home v2) are solid fills with 28pt corners and 20pt padding, with no border and no shadow; heroes sit directly on the paper with no card. R16 Fit **(changed)**. Main-app screens scroll. On iPhone 17 Pro at the default text size, each screen's most important element (e.g. Home's recovery score or state) is fully visible without scrolling.
R17 Digits. monospacedDigit only on numbers that animate. Static text like "Bedtime around 11:00 PM" uses proportional digits.
R18 Data honesty **(new)**. Every number on screen comes from an engine with a unit test. No number is hardcoded or estimated in a view. Missing data shows a stated empty state, never a placeholder number. **(changed)** Engines return values, never sentences with numbers in them: `InsightEngine` returns evidence, and `RecoveryEngine` returns `topContributor`. Words are written in `HomeScreenBuilder`. "Your normal range" means your usual ± 1 SD of your own last 14 nights (`RecoveryNormalRange`, with floored spreads), in every place it appears: the detail sentences, Home's "why" line and the sparklines.

R20 Architecture **(new)**. Views only read a view model's snapshot. No HealthKit calls, date math or scoring inside views. **(changed, Home v4)** The snapshot carries its render model: no sorting, date math or path building in a `body` or a `Canvas` closure (R23).
R21 Adaptive width **(new)**. No layout may assume a fixed screen width: iOS 27 makes iPhone apps resizable (iPhone Mirroring, iPad), and foldable displays exist.
- At regular horizontal size class, Home uses two columns: the hero and night panel on the left, the other blocks on the right. The pair stops growing at 1100pt.
- Otherwise content is at most 680pt wide, centered. This applies to Home, detail screens and "Coming soon".
- Rings and charts size from the width they're offered (`NoctisRingLayout`, flexible bars), never from device constants; stroke widths and center text scale with them.
- Rows of side-by-side labels move to a grid rather than truncate.
- Test at 320, 390, 440, 700 and 1000pt: `HomeWidthTests` renders exactly those widths off-screen, or use a resizable window or iPad simulator.
R22 Home charts **(new, Home v4)**. The sleep landscape, the ring's ticks and the week scrub are drawn to these numbers, and `NightPlotTests`, `RecoveryUsualTests` and `WeekScrubTests` hold them.
- Sleep landscape (night panel and share card, one component). One continuous ribbon through the real stage timeline. Each run is a capsule in its stage's lane, 12pt thick (60% of the lane if that is smaller), extended by half its thickness at both ends so neighbors overlap and joins merge. Each transition is a vertical capsule of the same thickness in the color of the stage entered, from the previous lane to the next. A run under 3 minutes (whole seconds) is a 6pt rounded notch in its lane; no run draws narrower than 4pt; Awake is never a tall stick. Under the ribbon, a stepped area: each run's color at .10 alpha from the ribbon down to the Deep lane line, flat, no gradient. Lane guides are 1pt ivory .08. The reveal draws the ribbon left to right with a 6pt ivory head dot that travels with it and fades at the end; the heart strip and stage totals fade in after, all inside the existing 2.1s reveal.
- Ring "usual" ticks. `RecoveryEngine` returns each factor's `usual`: the mean of that factor's score over the same 14-night baseline window, with the same 5-night minimum (nil below). Each ring segment gets a 3pt radial tick across its track at the usual level (ink .7), and one 12pt caption "Tick = your usual" sits under the factor capsules, only when a baseline exists. VoiceOver reads "Sleep 67, usual 74".
- This week's scrub. **(changed, Sleep)** One horizontal pan plus a tap (`columnScrub`, R24) over the whole chart maps x to the nearest night; the selection follows the finger with a snap haptic per bar change; the pill follows and clamps; the last selection stays until it is tapped again. There are no per-bar hit areas. Bars are fully saturated in both modes, `min(column − 10, 26)` wide with a 6pt radius; a selection dims the others to .45; last night is marked by a bold weekday initial and a 4pt dot, never by dimming.
- Evening greeting: "Good evening." before midnight, "Good night." from midnight to 4 AM, the same lavender accent.
R23 Home performance **(new, Home v4 part 2)**. Held by `HomePerformanceTests` (unit) and `HomePerformanceQATests` (UI); the device budgets are in the last bullet.
- Render model. Every piece of geometry (ring arcs and ticks, the night's runs, steps and heart line, the week's bars, the sparklines) is computed once per snapshot on `HomeRepository`'s actor into `HomeRenderModel` (NoctisCore, `Codable`, `Equatable`), carried by `HomeSnapshot.render`. Views scale it into points with one multiplication. Paths are built once per (render model, size) in an `onChange` or `onGeometryChange` handler, never in a `body` or a `Canvas` closure (`NightPaths`, `NoctisRingPaths`, `SparklinePaths`), and a lint test reads the sources to keep it so.
- Cached layers. Night panel: the ribbon (guides, area, steps, runs, notches) is one `Equatable` `.drawingGroup` layer and the heart strip another; the scrub line, dot and pill are plain views over them and the lane dim is an overlay, so scrubbing never redraws the ribbon. Ring: the tracks are one layer and the fills with their usual ticks another; the reveal animates one number, the angle of a pie mask that sweeps the fill layer, and changed values cross-fade between two static rings (a spring on two opacities). Only `dim` (a capsule's highlight) redraws the ring.
- Invalidation. A minute passing may re-evaluate only the countdown text, the "now" marker and the tab accessory: Home's root, its cards and the top overlay never read `homeClock` (the lint test lists the three files that may). The view model doesn't republish equal values (`screen`, `header`, `snapshot`), the hero's collapse progress and the compact bar's flag are separate properties, and a scroll writes state only when a derived value changes (a 1% step, the bar's 70%/65% thresholds). The DEBUG probe (`-noctisDebugProbe`) counts body evaluations for the tests.
- Saved snapshot. `HomeSnapshotStore` keeps the last snapshot in Application Support (`Noctis/home-snapshot.json`): derived values only, never a raw HealthKit record, a per-sample timestamp, the stage timeline or a source or device name; complete file protection, excluded from backups, registered with `NoctisDataRegistry` so Data Management erases it (ROADMAP.md). It is dropped when unreadable, from another schema or recovery model, older than 30 days or from the future; data over a day old comes back `.stale`; permission loss, no Health and no data erase it. A cold launch draws it in the first frame and revalidates: what changed cross-fades, and the day's reveal waits for fresh data (up to 1.5s) so it never plays yesterday's numbers. A screen without a score never spends the day's reveal. The mock provider (DEBUG) skips the file unless `-noctisSnapshotCache` asks.
- Interruptible motion. Every animation in Home is a `NoctisMotion` spring (`select`, `fade`, `settle`, `flow`, `snap`, `draw(duration)` with no bounce, or `reduced` under Reduce Motion): they retarget from where they are. No `.easeIn`, `.easeOut`, `.easeInOut` or `.linear` in HomeFeature (the ambient skeleton pulse is `NoctisMotion.pulse` and is removed when content arrives).
- Low Power Mode (`HomeViewModel.isLowPower`, following `ProcessInfo`): no score count-up (the score shows its end state), no capsule or week-bar staggers, no skeleton pulse or glyph draw-ons; the ring, night and dusk still play, and every end state is unchanged. Reduce Motion is unaffected. DEBUG `-noctisLowPowerMode` forces it (the simulator can't turn it on).
- Shadows. Cards keep the one live shadow (R2). Whether it costs an offscreen pass is a question for the Core Animation instrument on a device; only if it does, replace it with a pre-rendered 9-slice.
- Signposts and budgets. `NoctisSignpost` intervals in subsystem `com.noctis.app`, category `Performance`: `LaunchToFirstFrame`, `LaunchToPopulated`, `Reveal`, `Morph`, `Collapse` (a scroll, finger down to rest), `Scrub`, `TabSwitch` (to the first frame after the new content appears). Budgets on a real device: hitch time ratio under 5 ms/s during scroll, reveal, morph, scrub and tab switch; cold launch to Home's first frame under 400 ms and fully populated under 1.2 s; 120 fps sustained on ProMotion; no memory growth after 20 tab switches and 5 Recovery zoom cycles. Simulator numbers are never reported against them.
R24 Sleep **(new)**. Held by `SleepEngineTests`, `SleepRepositoryTests`, `SleepScreenTests`, `SleepRangeContentTests` (unit) and `SleepQATests` (UI).
- Engines (`SleepAnalysisKit`, nonisolated, deterministic). Metrics per night; quality `sleep-quality-v1` (duration 35, continuity 25, consistency 20, stages 20: a factor the data can't support is dropped and the rest renormalized; personal stage shares from 7 or more nights of stages, otherwise typical ranges, called "typical range"; confidence tiers as Recovery's); balance (asleep minus need summed over 7 nights, needing 5 recorded, "behind" or "ahead", never "debt"); consistency (sample SD of bedtime, wake time and duration over 7, 14 and 30 nights, each needing 5 recorded, plus the sleep midpoint); timing (medians of the last 14 nights, the recent shift, and a suggested range only from 14 of the last 30 nights). Naps are never nights. Every number on the tab is an engine's (R18); `SleepScreenBuilder` and `SleepRangeContent` write the words.
- Wording. Nights are compared with the person's own need and pattern. Never a claim to measure health, diagnose or warn; nothing red; each detail sheet (Consistency, Balance, Timing) shows the number, a chart, the calculation in plain words and ends "Patterns matter more than perfect nights."
- Render model. `SleepRenderModel` per selection (a night and Day, Week or Month), built on `SleepRepository`'s actor and cached: geometry in unit space, views scale it (R23). Lines are `Shape`s over unit-space points (`SleepSmoothLine`); nothing is built in a `Canvas` closure.
- One bars card serves Week and Month (`SleepBarsCard`), keyed by night, so the segmented switch regroups the same bars with springs instead of rebuilding the chart.
- Charts never take a vertical drag from the page. Choosing a night on a chart is a horizontal pan that only begins when the finger moves more sideways than up or down (`HorizontalScrubGesture`) plus a tap (`columnScrub`): the pan follows the finger with a snap haptic per column, a tap picks a column, a tap on the picked one clears it. Never `DragGesture(minimumDistance: 0)` over scrolling content: it claims the touch and the page stops scrolling.
- The selection pill has its own 28pt row above the bars (wrapping upward at accessibility sizes) and never covers a bar.
- Reveal. Once per calendar day per tab (`noctis.sleep.lastIntroDay`), on the first Day view with data: the ribbon draws, the ring fills, the numbers count up; any touch skips it; Reduce Motion and Low Power Mode as R23.
- States reuse Home's copy and patterns: no permission and no Health ("Your first night starts here."), no data, learning under 5 nights (dots in place of the ring and the quality card), a stale banner, a skeleton shaped like the day, an error with "Try again". A night with nothing recorded is a "No data" bar or an empty card, never a zero or "--"; a night without stages is one bar with a caption saying why, and drops its stage rows and the stages factor.
R25 Recovery **(new)**. Held by `RecoveryHistoryTests`, `RecoveryContributionTests`, `RecoveryRepositoryTests`, `RecoveryFactorTests`, `RecoveryScreenTests` (unit) and `RecoveryQATests` (UI).
- Engines (`RecoveryKit`, nonisolated, deterministic). The score is `RecoveryEngine` (model V3: sleep 35, HRV 20, resting heart rate 25, consistency 20; HRV and resting heart rate scored by z against the person's own baseline; a heart-rate cap when both are 1.5 SD or more off). `RecoveryHistoryEngine` scores every past night as of that morning (no lookahead) and gives the stats (current, average, baseline, high and low with their days, trend as a least-squares slope in points a week: 1.5 or more is Rising, -1.5 or less is Lower, otherwise Steady) and the usual range (baseline plus or minus the larger of one SD and 3 points). `RecoveryContributionEngine` splits a score into whole points per factor (largest remainder, so the rows add to the total). `RecoveryConfidenceReport` says what the score rests on and what would add the rest. Every number on the tab is an engine's (R18); `RecoveryScreenBuilder`, `RecoveryHistoryContent` and `RecoveryFactorContent` write the words.
- Wording. Where a value sits is "Above", "Within" or "Below your usual". Never "poor", "bad", "you should rest" or "you are recovered". The trend is a direction with an arrow and a number a week, never red. "In your usual range on 11 of 14 days." A night without a score is a gap in the line, never a zero.
- Render model. `RecoveryRenderModel` per snapshot, built on `RecoveryRepository`'s actor: the ring and the build bar for Today, and unit-space lines, bands and weekly averages for each range (R23). A factor's line is resampled to a fixed 120 samples so a range change morphs one shape (`FactorCurve`) with a spring. Views scale and draw; nothing is built in a `Canvas`.
- Segments. Today, 7D, 30D and 90D on one view model (`RecoveryViewModel.selection`), a factor's detail being a selection on the same model; a deep link is `noctis://recovery?range=30d&factor=hrv`. 7D shows every night as a dot; 90D shows a 7-night smoothed line, weekly averages, and today's dot joined to the line it sits against.
- Charts never take a vertical drag from the page: `columnScrub` (R24), with the pill "Tue, Sep 29 · Recovery 76 · Sleep 7h 12m". Free vs Premium gating is in ROADMAP.md; all of Recovery is open until the paywall decides.
- Reveal. Once per calendar day per tab (`noctis.recovery.lastIntroDay`), on the first Today or history view with data: the ring fills and the numbers count up, or the line draws left to right and its dots land as it arrives; any touch skips it; Reduce Motion and Low Power Mode as R23.
- States reuse Home's and Sleep's copy: no permission and no Health ("Your first night starts here."), no data, learning under 5 nights (dots), no sleep last night (a gap), stale ("Apple Health hasn't updated since ..." with Open Health, and the history under it), a skeleton shaped like the segment, an error with "Try again". Sleep only is a two-segment ring at low confidence and its card says why and what would add the rest.
- Routing. Home's Recovery hero and evening card open Recovery on Today with the zoom; a factor capsule opens that factor's page; a second press of the Recovery tab scrolls it to the top; a factor row on a range opens that factor on the same range.

R30 Night **(new; Night part 1: bedtime and the wake alarm)**. Held by `BedtimeRecommendationTests`, `BedtimeInputsTests`, `TonightClockTests`, `AlarmStateMachineTests`, `AlarmServiceTests`, `AlarmKitBackendTests` and `AlarmConfigurationTests` (unit). (The spec asked for R27; R27 to R29 were taken.)
- The principle: never claim a capability the platform doesn't give. There is no "smart wake", no sleep-stage wake-up, no "we'll wake you at the best moment": an alarm rings at the time that was set, and the copy calls it an "Alarm". Nothing may promise more than AlarmKit does.
- The model (`NightKit`, nonisolated, deterministic; `BedtimeModel.v1` = "bedtime-v1", every number in one `BedtimeParameters`). Times are minutes since midnight and every difference is taken on the clock's circle, so midnight never breaks them (`BedtimeClock`). (1) Base = wake time − sleep need (− the clock change, below). (2) Catch-up: a 7-night balance 60 or more minutes behind moves the center earlier by a quarter of it, to the nearest 5, at most 30; ahead or in line changes nothing. (3) Gradual: with a usual bedtime (the median of the last 14 nights, 7+ nights), a center more than 45 minutes from it is held at 45 minutes from it on the side of the target. (4) Window = center ± 15 minutes, each end to the nearest 5. Under 7 nights it is one bedtime, the base, with no window ("A starting point. We're still learning your pattern."). Confidence has Recovery's tiers: high needs all four inputs (need, wake time, balance, usual bedtime) and 14 nights; moderate needs three and 7 nights; less is low. Reasons are the facts with their values (`BedtimeReason`); the words are the screen's (R18).
- Daylight saving: on the night the clocks change, 8 hours of sleep is 9 hours on the clock going forward and 7 coming back. `TonightClock` reads the real offsets at bedtime and waking, and the engine takes `clockChangeMinutes` (+60 or −60 on that night, 0 otherwise): the base moves by it and expected sleep is the clock gap less it. Nothing else in the model touches a calendar.
- The alarm (`AlarmService`, an actor over `AlarmBackend`; `AlarmKitBackend` is the real one, the DEBUG `MockAlarmBackend` plays one). `AlarmStateMachine` is one pure function over `AlarmState` (off, scheduled, armed, snoozed, firing, dismissed, cancelled, permissionDenied, unavailable, error) and `AlarmEvent`; every transition that exists is in `AlarmStateMachineTests` and every other pair is proved ignored. `AlarmConfiguration` (SwiftData, beside the profile, local: the app has no CloudKit path) is the alarm's own record, because the system deletes a one-shot alarm the moment it has rung: an alarm we believe is armed that the system no longer lists has rung ("dismissed").
- What AlarmKit actually is, checked against the iOS SDK (not the spec's assumptions): there is no "update" call, so changing an alarm is a cancel and a new schedule; its states are scheduled, countdown, paused and alerting, so "snoozed" is the countdown after a Snooze button whose behavior is `.countdown` with a post-alert duration, and "dismissed", "armed" and "cancelled" are ours; a countdown presentation without a countdown duration is refused, so with no snooze there is neither; the countdown is drawn by the app's widget extension (`NoctisAlarmWidget`, Night part 3); the permission is asked by `requestAuthorization()` or by the first schedule, and the Info.plist must carry `NSAlarmKitUsageDescription`; the only error type is `maximumLimitReached`. It exists from iOS 26, which is the app's minimum, so "AlarmKit is missing" can't happen here: `unavailable` is for a system that says it can't (and for the mock).
- The screen (`NightFeature`, on `TabScreenScaffold` with the lavender field, pushed over Home, never a tab). Tonight reads only `TonightViewModel`: the bedtime from the same snapshot as the Sleep tab (so it works with no Health data too, as one starting bedtime), and the alarm from `AlarmService`. The field has the date and "Bedtime window 10:45 – 11:15 PM" (or "Bedtime around 11:00 PM"); under it a card with the window at 44pt (`NoctisMetric`), "Wake 7:00 AM · 8h need" and the 6 PM to 10 AM strip (the window in lavender, a fainter span to waking, the wake marker); then "Why this time" (a tile row per fact, what the catch-up did in words, and "How this is calculated", which names bedtime-v1 and gets its numbers from `BedtimeParameters`); then "Start wind-down" (the 58pt cocoa capsule); then the alarm card (the time, an On/Off toggle, the days, a state chip: Scheduled, Snoozed, Ringing, Off, Permission needed, Not available, Couldn't set); then the Sleep sounds row. Wake time is the profile's; the alarm's own time is the alarm's, and the two are not tied.
- The alarm sheet. A wheel for the time, seven 44pt day circles (Monday first), Snooze with 5, 9 or 10 minutes, and one honest line, "Rings at your set time, even in silent mode or Focus.", pinned with the button over a paper fade that shows only while something is under it (R14). Before the first alarm the sheet says what is about to be asked, and the button reads "Continue"; the system's prompt comes after it, once. Permission refused: the sheet is the message and "Open Settings", nothing else (the toggle stays off, and the honest line is not shown because nothing can ring). Unavailable: a note that alarms need iOS 26 or later, and the card is replaced by it. An error: the message and "Try again" with the sheet still editable.
- Routing. `AppRoute.tonight`, `.windDown(autostart:)`, `.sounds` and `.alarm` (`noctis://tonight`, `noctis://wind-down?autostart=1`, `noctis://sounds`, `noctis://alarm`) all open over Home: Wind-down and Sounds over Tonight, the alarm as Tonight with its sheet open. R31 Sounds **(new; Night, sleep sounds)**. Held by `SoundSynthTests`, `SoundRendererTests`, `SoundRealtimeSafetyTests`, `SoundPlaybackPolicyTests`, `SleepTimerPlanTests` and `SoundPlayerTests` (unit). (The spec asked for R28; R27 to R30 were taken.)
- The principle: every sound is synthesized in code with AVAudioEngine and an `AVAudioSourceNode`. No audio file is shipped: no recording, no sample, no loop. Forest and Night are not in the app because they would need real recordings (ROADMAP.md).
- Real-time safety is mandatory. The render callback never allocates, takes a lock, calls into Swift concurrency or touches an actor. `SoundRenderer` keeps all of its state in one block of memory reached through a pointer, mixes six value-type voices with plain `while` loops, and takes its orders from a mailbox of atomics (the version number written last, read twice, so a half-written command waits for the next buffer). The sources have no generics, no closures, no `Array`, no class property to police. `SoundRealtimeSafetyTests` proves it by counting heap allocations on the rendering thread with `malloc_logger` (with a deliberate allocation as proof the hook counts) while all six sounds render, cross, fade and change gain; a second test scans the render files for the words that allocate or wait. It found a real fault on the first run: `for i in 0..<n` is generic iteration, which allocates on every pass in a Debug build (it specialises away in Release, but a real-time path does not depend on the optimiser), so the render path uses `while`.
- What the SDK says: iOS 27 adds `AVAudioSourceNodeRenderBlockRealtimeSafe` and marks it unavailable in Swift ("Swift is not supported for use with audio realtime threads"). The ordinary `AVAudioSourceNodeRenderBlock` is what Swift can use, and what this uses. If a hard guarantee is ever needed, the render core is the part to move to C. `installTap` is deprecated from iOS 27 for a throwing variant that has no Swift spelling yet (it is only `__installTap`); the old one is used and does not warn at the iOS 26 floor.
- The sounds (`SoundSources.swift`, 44.1 kHz, two channels from two seeds so they surround the head): Brown noise (white noise leaked through an integrator), Rain (pink noise high-passed, and short band-passed droplets from a pool of six, at a low gain), Ocean (pink and brown through a low-pass that opens and closes with a swell of nine to twelve seconds, a drifting cutoff and a touch of foam), Wind (band-passed noise with a wandering center and slow random gusts), Fireplace (a flickering low rumble and randomly timed crackles, mostly small, sometimes a pair), Ambient (a quiet fixed chord of D, A, E and F sharp over a low D, each note three sines a few cents apart, each breathing on its own slow cycle, through a low-pass that moves over half a minute). Each has a seeded random source (`SoundRandom`), so `SoundSynth.render` gives the same samples for the same seed. Levels are set so every sound peaks under 0.7 at natural level and under 0.95 at full gain, with no offset (the largest measured is 0.0004), and a DC blocker runs in every source.
- The player (`SoundPlayer`, the one object that holds the engine, main-actor) reads like this: `SoundPlaybackPolicy` is one pure function of phase and event (stopped, playing, paused, interrupted). A call, Siri or an alarm pauses at once and resumes by itself only if the system says `shouldResume` and the person hadn't paused first; unplugged headphones pause for good (a private sound must not move to the speaker); a media services reset rebuilds the engine. Each sound has its own gain (default 40%, kept in UserDefaults), a change of sound crosses over 1.5 s in equal power, play, pause and stop are 0.3 s ramps, and the sleep timer (15, 30, 45, 60, 90 minutes) is a plan of plain dates (`SleepTimerPlan`) whose last 20 seconds are a fade, ending in a clean stop: engine stopped and let go, session deactivated, Now Playing cleared. Nothing runs when nothing plays: no timer task, no tap, no engine.
- The system: `AVAudioSession` `.playback` without `.mixWithOthers`; `UIBackgroundModes` audio so a sound plays on with the screen locked; Now Playing (the sound's name, "NOCTIS", no elapsed time, marked live) and the play, pause and toggle commands of the lock screen and headphones, with next, previous and seeking turned off. The level meter is a tap on the main mixer that reports a smoothed RMS 15 times a second, installed only while a sound is playing and the Sounds screen asks for it.
- The screen (`NightFeature`, on `TabScreenScaffold` with the sage field, pushed over Tonight; `AppRoute.sounds`). Title "Sleep sounds", the caption "STEADY SOUNDS FOR WINDING DOWN" and the summary "Made on your iPhone. Nothing downloads." A grid of six tiles (two columns, each 56pt flat `NoctisIconTile` in its block color: Brown noise cocoa, Rain lavender, Ocean sage, Wind butter, Fireplace orange, Ambient apricot); the one that is playing has a 2pt cocoa border and a three-bar level glyph. A tap plays a sound, crossing from the one playing; a tap on the playing tile pauses it. The Now Playing card is pinned above the tab bar over a paper fade that shows only while content is under it: its tile and name, play and pause (44pt), a 24-bar waveform of the real meter (RMS in decibels from -50 to -10 dBFS, newest on the right, cocoa, no glow; a steady sound such as rain gives an even row, and ocean, wind and fire show their movement), a gain slider for that sound, and the timer button, which shows the time left (numericText) and opens a sheet of 15, 30, 45, 60, 90 minutes and Off. When the timer runs out, one soft haptic and "Sleep well." Reduce Motion: the waveform is one static level bar and the tile glyph stands still; no other motion.
- Copy: the sounds are described as what they are ("Steady sounds for winding down") and never for what they do for anyone; `SoundsContentTests` lints every string against health and sleep claims. An interruption reads "Paused by iPhone" and the play button resumes.
- Nothing runs when the screen is away: the meter's tap and the countdown's tick are on only while the Sounds screen is showing (and the app is active) and a sound plays; the sound itself plays on behind the lock screen.
- DEBUG: `-mockSound silent|playing:rain|interrupted:ocean|timer:rain:30|real:wind`. Without `real:`, a silent engine renders the real sounds for the meter and nothing reaches a speaker, so screenshots and UI tests are quiet.

R32 Wind-down **(new; Night, the wind-down session)**. Held by `WindDownMachineTests`, `WindDownClockTests`, `WindDownVisualsTests`, `WindDownCountdownTests`, `WindDownViewModelTests`, `WindDownSceneTests` and `NoctisNightTests` (unit).

Progress `p` (0 to 1) is read from the clock (the time since the start less the time paused, over the planned 10, 20 or 30 minutes; 20 is the default and the last choice is remembered), never counted in ticks or frames: the view model reads it once a second while running, and coming back to the front reads it at once, so a session that ran on behind the lock screen is exactly where it should be, and one that would have ended while away ends. Nothing ticks while paused or once it is over.
- Everything that moves is a function of `p` (`WindDownVisuals`, tested): 12 stars at the start and 24 by the end, fading in and never going back; the moon along a gentle arc, up from the left, topping it in the middle, settling at the right; the hills sinking 24pt; the background from night to deep. The controls simplify in three stages (`full` below 0.3, `reduced` below 0.6, `minimal` from 0.6: a small End and the sound toggle), and the durations of those changes lengthen as `p` rises (0.35 s at the start, 1.55 s at the end). Nothing is in a `TimelineView` and nothing runs faster than the one-second read. Reduce Motion shows five checkpoints (0, .25, .5, .75, 1) as plain 0.2 s fades with no travel.
- The screen stays awake while a session runs (the idle timer is off) and goes back to normal when it ends, however it ends. Locking the phone keeps the session and any sound going (background audio, R31). Ending, by time or by the person, gives one soft haptic and fades the sound over four seconds before stopping it cleanly (`SoundPlayer.fadeOutAndStop`).
- What is kept (`WindDownSession`, SwiftData, local, beside the profile): when it began, how long it was planned for, and whether it ran its whole time. Nothing else. `AppRoute.windDown(autostart: true)` starts a session as it opens (the Siri-ready entry point); `autostart: false` (the notification, Tonight, Home's evening card) opens the pre-start state.
- The screen (`WindDownScreen`, `WindDownScene`, `DesignSystem/NoctisNight.swift`): full-screen, no scaffold, no tab bar. It does not use the adaptive `NoctisAppColor` tokens, because `.preferredColorScheme(.dark)` does not recolor those inside a pushed view and the screen must look the same in system Light and Dark: it uses the fixed `NoctisNight` colors (night `#17110E` to deep `#0E0A08`, ivory ink, ivory-2 for quiet words, lavender `#8E7BDC`, moon ivory, two lighter-cocoa hills), contrast-tested on both ends of the sky, and the countdown's `.nightPanel` metric tone. The sky is one `Canvas` drawn from `p`. Stars are placed once in the strips of sky no state's words or controls reach (`WindDownScene.skyBands`, tested), so none sits behind a letter or shows through a chip. Pre-start: duration chips (10, 20, 30; 44pt), an optional sound, Begin and a quiet Not now. Running: the countdown (64pt, minutes until the last minute, then seconds), the action row while `p` is below 0.3 (Sounds, Breathing and Meditation; Reflect appears when it is built, R19), then the controls simplify to a small End and the sound toggle by 0.6. An action opens over the same night (`WindDownScene(backdrop: true)`: the same sky color and hills, the moon at rest at the top right beside the way back, and stars only above that row, so nothing is drawn behind the words and controls that fill the rest of the screen) and closes back to it without pausing the clock. Finish: "Sleep well.", the alarm's status or a Set alarm button, and Done. Reduce Motion: the same states as plain fades at the five checkpoints, no travel (`-mockWindDown running:0.5:reduce` shows any of them).
- DEBUG: `-mockWindDown prestart|running:0.3|running:0.5:reduce|finished` (`running:1` ends a session the moment it opens, for the finish card at the end of the sky). In a zsh script write `${p}:reduce`, not `$p:reduce`: zsh reads `:r` after a variable as "strip the extension".

R33 Breathing **(new; Night, opened from the wind-down)**. Held by `BreathingPatternTests`, `BreathingScheduleTests`, `BreathingHapticPlanTests`, `SessionClockTests`, `BreathingCopyTests` and `BreathingViewModelTests` (unit).
- A slow breathing exercise, and nothing more said about it: "A slow breathing exercise." No claim about anxiety, stress, mood, sleep or health, no score, no streak, and a test over the words (`BreathingCopyTests`) holds that line.
- The patterns live in one config (`BreathingPattern.all`, `NightKit`): Calm 4-2-6, Box 4-4-4-4, Relax 4-7-8, each four whole-second numbers (inhale, hold full, exhale, hold empty). The lengths are 2, 5 and 10 minutes, and the exercise is whole breaths: it ends at the end of the breath that reaches the chosen length, never short of it and never mid-breath (Box 2 min is 128 s, Relax 5 min is 304 s; the screen says "5 min left", to the nearest minute, so it never reads 6 at the start of a 5 minute exercise).
- `BreathingSchedule.moment(at:)` is a pure function of the seconds elapsed: the phase, its countdown, how full the lungs are. A boundary belongs to the phase that begins there (at exactly 4 s of a 4-2-6 breath it is the hold), tested at every boundary of every pattern, and by walking each whole exercise a quarter second at a time. The clock (`SessionClock`, shared with Meditation) is the time since the start less the time paused; the view model reads it at every whole second of the exercise, which is where every phase changes, so coming back from the lock screen is exact and an exercise that would have ended while away ends.
- The circle is one flat shape (ivory at 15% with an ivory outline, 300pt at most): at each phase it is animated to that phase's end level over exactly the time the phase has left, on `timingCurve(1/3, 0, 2/3, 1)`, which is the scheduler's own smoothstep, so a circle caught up mid-phase and the numbers agree. Nothing is in a `TimelineView`. Reduce Motion replaces the circle with a level bar that steps once a second. The phase name and its seconds count down inside it ("Inhale 4"), and VoiceOver is told each phase as it begins ("Inhale, 4 seconds") and "Finished".
- Haptics are optional and OFF until switched on, and the switch is only offered on a device that can play them (`CHHapticEngine.capabilitiesForHardware().supportsHaptics`). `BreathingHapticPlan` says what each phase does (a gentle swell across an inhale, one soft tick at a hold, a fade across an exhale), as numbers a test can check; `CoreHapticsBreathing` plays them, haptics only so it never touches the audio session the wind-down's sound is on. The SDK gives no way to read the System Haptics setting, so the app does not pretend to: the system keeps them quiet when the person has them off (to be checked on a real iPhone). A failure to play is silent and logged by outcome only.
- Pausing freezes the circle where it is and stops the haptics; leaving the screen, or the wind-down ending, ends the exercise and leaves nothing running; the wind-down's own clock runs on throughout.
- DEBUG: `-mockBreathing prestart[:box][:haptics]|running:calm:5[:paused][:2min][:haptics]|finished`; `:paused` holds one moment so a screenshot can look at it, and `:haptics` swaps in a silent engine that says it has them so the switch can be seen on a simulator. `-mockWindDown running:0.1:breathing` opens it over the session.

R34 Meditation **(new; Night, opened from the wind-down)**. Held by `MeditationScheduleTests`, `ChimeSynthTests`, `MeditationCatalogTests` and `MeditationViewModelTests` (unit).
- Quiet, timed sessions: Sleep, Relax, Breathing, Body scan and Quiet mind, for 5, 10 or 20 minutes (the length is exact). Calm and plain, and nothing said about what it does for anyone's health, mind or sleep: the words are held by a test over the whole catalog and the screen's own copy (`MeditationCatalogTests`: no anxiety, stress, treatment, therapy, medical, "fall asleep", "sleep better", guarantee, proof or study).
- `MeditationSchedule(category:minutes:)` (`NightKit`) is a pure list of cues. A start chime at 0 and a closing chime at the end; the lines evenly spaced from a six second lead-in to a 24 second quiet tail (a body scan walks all twelve places from the head to the feet, 22.5, 47.5 or 97.5 seconds each; the others say 6, 9 or 12 of their twelve lines, sampled evenly so the arc from first to last is kept and none repeats); and a soft transition chime as the second and third part begin (at two thirds of the lines, and at thirds of the time for Quiet mind, which says nothing at all and only has a ring, its name and the chimes). Tested for every category at every length: totals, order, even gaps, the tail, the chimes' places.
- The lines and the category names are in the String Catalog `NightFeature/Resources/Meditation.xcstrings` (English now; 58 strings), read with `MeditationCopy.string(_:)` the way Coach's are, and a test fails if any key is missing. A line on screen is the latest begun; before the first there is none.
- The view model reads the clock (`SessionClock`, shared with Breathing) at every whole second and at every cue, never counts. A cue that is past its time by more than three seconds (the app was away) is not played late: the line on screen is the right one, and a cue just missed is still sounded. Pausing holds the clock, stops the voice and sounds no cue; ending or leaving it leaves nothing running, and the closing chime is let ring before the audio session is let go.
- The chimes are made in code (`ChimeSynth`, `NightKit`): one sine, a 20 ms rise, an exponential decay to -60 dB and a 50 ms release to exactly zero (C5 to begin, E5 for a change of part, a lower, longer G4 to close; a third of full scale at the loudest), held as in-memory 16-bit WAV data and played with `AVAudioPlayer`. No audio file ships (R31). Tested: pitch, softness, no clipping, a zero start and end, a monotone decay, a valid WAV header. Its audio session mixes with other audio and is only brought up here when no sleep sound has already done it.
- "Spoken guidance" is OFF until switched on, and only offered where the device has a system voice. It is `AVSpeechSynthesizer` with the system voice (a slow rate, a second after any chime), and says so on the screen: "Read aloud by your iPhone's built-in voice. The pauses follow the schedule." Each line is spoken once, as it begins; between lines there is silence, so the pauses are the schedule's.
- "Sound underneath" is optional and not offered when a sound is already playing (the wind-down's own). A chosen sound plays at a quiet 0.2 for the session, fades out over four seconds at the end however it ends, and the person's own volume for that sound (the player keeps one per sound) is put back once it is quiet; "Again" while the last one is still fading starts the new sound clean.
- The screen: a slow progress ring that fills once over the session (one linear second at a time, never a timer drawing it; Reduce Motion steps it), the time left in it, and one line at a time that fades into the next over 0.9 s (0.2 s plain under Reduce Motion). VoiceOver is told each line as it begins and "Finished".
- DEBUG: `-mockMeditation prestart[:bodyScan][:10min][:spoken]|running:bodyScan:10:80[:paused]|finished[:relax][:5min]`; always silent (no mock scenario or `-mockMeditation` ever makes a sound), `:spoken` says there is a voice to offer, `:paused` holds one moment. `-mockWindDown running:0.1:meditation` opens it over the session.
