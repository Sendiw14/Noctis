Privacy Policy

In short. NOCTIS has no server, no account of its own, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code. Your sleep data is read from Apple Health on your iPhone and stays there. We do not collect it and we cannot see it.

What NOCTIS reads

With your permission, NOCTIS reads sleep, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and respiratory rate from Apple Health. It only reads. It never writes to Health. It keeps no copy of the readings; it keeps numbers worked out from them (for example your recovery score and bedtime plan) on your iPhone.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Summaries worked out from your Health data, and the insights and reviews built from them.
  • Your profile and settings: sleep need, wake time, goals, preferences, and the name you choose to enter. Your name is never shown to Coach.
  • Your wake alarm, wind-down history, sound and meditation choices.
  • Your journal and your Coach conversations. Nothing else in the app can read your journal.
  • Coach writes its answers on your iPhone: with Apple’s on-device model where your iPhone supports it and Apple Intelligence is on, and from fixed wording otherwise. Your questions and its answers are not sent to us or to any other service. Its answers are not medical advice.
  • If you use Sign in with Apple (optional): an identifier Apple gives NOCTIS, kept in your iPhone’s Keychain. NOCTIS asks for your name only, never your email.

The widgets and the Apple Watch app show a short summary of your recovery and sleep. The summary is shared between NOCTIS and its widgets on your iPhone, and sent to your own paired Apple Watch. It is never sent anywhere else.

What leaves your iPhone

  • To us: nothing. There is no server to send it to.
  • Purchases: NOCTIS Premium is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, restore and refunds. We receive no record of your purchase from the app.
  • Contact support: if you tap it and send the email, the message goes to our support address with your app version, build, iOS version and what you wrote.
  • Export and share: if you export your data or share a night, the file or picture goes where you choose to send it.

Tracking and advertising

NOCTIS does not track you, show ads, or share data with data brokers or advertising companies.

Your choices

  • Turn off NOCTIS’s access to Health at any time in the Health app or in Settings.
  • Delete one kind of data, reset your preferences, or delete everything in Settings, then Data management. "Delete account and data" also removes your Sign in with Apple identifier from NOCTIS.
  • Export your data from the same place.
  • Readings in Apple Health are removed in the Health app.

Medical notice

NOCTIS isn’t a medical device and doesn’t diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes we will change the date above and describe the change in the app’s release notes.
