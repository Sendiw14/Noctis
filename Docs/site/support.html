Common questions

NOCTIS shows no data.

In the Health app, open your profile, then Privacy, then Apps, then NOCTIS, and turn on Sleep, Heart Rate, Resting Heart Rate, Heart Rate Variability and Respiratory Rate. NOCTIS can only read what Apple Health holds, so a night needs sleep data from an Apple Watch or another source.

How do I delete my data?

Settings, then Data management. You can delete one kind of data, or choose "Delete all NOCTIS data". "Delete account and data" also removes your Sign in with Apple identifier from NOCTIS. NOCTIS never copies your Health readings, so those are removed in the Health app.

How do I export my data?

Settings, Data management, "Export my data".

How do I cancel or restore Premium?

Settings, then Subscription, "Manage subscription" opens your Apple Account subscriptions. "Restore Purchases" is on the same screen and on the paywall. Apple handles all payments and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

My Watch is out of date.

Open NOCTIS on the iPhone once. The iPhone sends the Watch a short summary of your recovery and sleep.

NOCTIS isn’t a medical device and doesn’t diagnose or treat any condition. If you are worried about your health, talk to a doctor.
